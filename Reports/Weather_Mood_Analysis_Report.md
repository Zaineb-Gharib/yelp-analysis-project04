## Executive Summary

This report investigates how weather conditions influence customer
behavior, sentiment, and business performance using Yelp data combined
with historical weather records.

Our findings show a clear pattern: **extreme heat negatively impacts
customer sentiment**, while **rain slightly increases both engagement
and ratings**. Businesses experience lower satisfaction scores during
very hot days, suggesting discomfort affects perception.

Rain, contrary to intuition, correlates with increased activity---likely
due to indoor dining preferences.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Methodology

### Data Integration

We combined Yelp business and review data with historical weather
datasets, aligning them by date and location.

### Feature Engineering

Weather variables included: - Temperature - Precipitation - Weather
conditions (rain, clear, etc.)

### Analysis Approach

We analyzed: - Average ratings vs temperature - Check-ins vs weather
conditions - Sentiment trends across time

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Key Findings

### Heat Reduces Ratings

Customer ratings drop during high temperatures. Extreme heat creates
discomfort that negatively affects perception.

### Rain Increases Engagement

Rainy days show higher check-ins and slightly better ratings. Customers
prefer indoor experiences during bad weather.

### Moderate Weather Is Optimal

Mild conditions produce the highest satisfaction and most stable
engagement.

## Critical Insights

### Insight 1: Weather Shapes Perception

Customer satisfaction is not purely based on service or food---it is
influenced by external environmental conditions.

### Insight 2: Heat Is a Hidden Risk

Businesses in hot climates face unseen challenges during peak
temperatures.

### Insight 3: Rain Is an Opportunity

Rainy days can be leveraged for promotions and increased indoor traffic.
